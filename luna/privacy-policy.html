Privacy Policy

Luna: Tarot & Horoscope (package com.cynora.luna) · Effective November 22, 2026

In short: Luna works mostly on your device. We do not sell your data. Readings are for entertainment and self-reflection only. If you sign in or post in the community, some data is stored in Google Firebase, and the app shows ads through Google AdMob.

1. Who we are

Luna is published by Cynorra ("we", "us"). Contact: support@cynorra.com or cynorra.app@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Stored on your device only

  • Your choices in the app: mood, focus area, language, theme, notification settings, which readings you have already seen, your streak and counters.
  • A random identifier generated by the app for local use.
  • Birth date, time and place that you type into the Birth Chart feature. They are used on your device to calculate the reading and are not uploaded to our servers.

2.2 Account (optional)

  • Anonymous sign-in. To use the community, the app creates an anonymous Firebase Authentication account. It only has a random user ID.
  • Google Sign-In (optional). If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication receives your Google account email address, display name, profile photo URL and a user ID. Your anonymous account is linked to it so your activity stays with you.

2.3 Community posts

If you share an intention in the Mystic Community, we store the text you write, an automatically assigned nickname and emoji, the time, like counts and the user ID of the author in Google Cloud Firestore. Posts and nicknames are visible to other users of the app. Do not write personal information, contact details or anything you do not want others to read.

3. Advertising and consent

Luna is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) so you can choose whether ads may be personalized. You can change your choice later in the app's privacy options where available, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. Google's practices are described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

4. Notifications

Luna can send daily and hourly reading notifications. On Android 13 and later the app asks for permission first. You can turn notifications off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

5. How we use data

  • To provide the features you use (readings, community, sign-in).
  • To keep the app secure and stable, and to prevent abuse.
  • To measure usage and show ads.

We do not sell personal data and we do not use your data for profiling beyond the advertising choices described above.

6. Sharing and processors

We share data only with service providers that run the app for us: Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging, AdMob). Data may be processed on Google servers outside your country. Google acts under its own terms and data protection commitments.

7. Retention

  • Local data stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.
  • Account and community data stay until you ask us to delete them (see section 8) or we remove them for abuse.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept by Google for the periods set in Firebase.

8. Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or to delete your account together with your community posts. Follow the steps on the account and data deletion page or email us. We reply within 30 days. If you are in the EEA, UK or Turkey you also have the rights granted by GDPR, UK GDPR and KVKK (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, and the right to complain to your data protection authority).

9. Children

Luna is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Entertainment disclaimer

Tarot, horoscope, dream, palm and other readings in Luna are generated for entertainment and personal reflection. They are not predictions and do not replace professional medical, legal or financial advice.

11. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS) and Firebase applies access rules to stored data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and material changes will be announced in the app or on this page.

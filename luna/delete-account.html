Account and Data Deletion

Luna: Tarot & Horoscope (com.cynora.luna)

Delete your account and community posts in the app (fastest)

Open Luna, go to Profile > About > Delete account and data and confirm. This deletes your community posts, your Firebase account and everything stored on your device immediately. If Android asks you to sign in again for security, sign out, sign in with Google again and repeat.

Delete everything Luna stores on your device

Most of Luna's data is only on your phone. Open Android Settings > Apps > Luna > Storage and tap Clear data, or uninstall the app. This removes your preferences, reading history, streak and the local identifier.

Or send us a deletion request

If you cannot use the app anymore (for example you uninstalled it), email us:

  1. Email support@cynorra.com (or cynorra.app@gmail.com) with the subject Luna account and data deletion.
  2. Write from the email address of the Google account you signed in with. If you only used the anonymous community, tell us the nickname and approximate time of your posts.
  3. We confirm the request and delete the data within 30 days.
What is deleted: your Firebase Authentication account (email, name, photo URL, user ID) and your community posts. The like counters on other people's posts may still include your like, but they are no longer linked to any account.

What may remain for a limited time: reports you made about other users' posts (kept so the people you reported can be protected), aggregated analytics and crash reports kept by Google in Firebase, and data Google AdMob processes under Google's own policies. Your advertising ID can be reset or deleted in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads.

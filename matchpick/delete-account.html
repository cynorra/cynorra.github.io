1. Data Deletion Policy Overview

In full compliance with Google Play's Data Safety Policy and privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, KVKK), users of MatchPick Pro (Package: com.cynnorra.matchpick) have the absolute right to permanently delete their account and all associated personal data.

You do not need to have the app installed on your device to complete this process.

2. What Data Is Deleted?

  • Authentication Profile: Your Google Sign-In UID, email address, display name, and avatar image link stored in Firebase Authentication.
  • Predictions & Scores: All match predictions submitted by your account, points earned, streak milestones, and referral association.
  • Community Contributions: All posts, comments, and like interactions published by your UID in the Community Feed.
  • Leaderboard Presence: Your profile is immediately and permanently removed from all global and regional rank leaderboards.
⚠️ Irreversible Action: Once your account is deleted, your prediction history, total points, and consecutive streaks cannot be recovered or restored under any circumstances.

3. Submit Deletion Request

Please provide the email address or Firebase User ID (UID) associated with your MatchPick Pro account. Your deletion request will be processed automatically within 24 to 48 hours.

✓ Your deletion request has been registered successfully. All associated account data and prediction records will be permanently purged from our Firebase Cloud Firestore servers within 24 hours. A confirmation has been logged.

4. Alternative Direct Contact

You can also initiate data deletion by sending an email directly from the email address registered with the app to:

  • Data Protection Support: support@cynorra.com or cynorra.app@gmail.com
  • Subject: MatchPick Pro Account Deletion Request

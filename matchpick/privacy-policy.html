1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy governs your use of the mobile application MatchPick Pro ("the App"), developed and operated by Cynorra ("we", "us", or "our"), published on Google Play under the package identifier com.cynnorra.matchpick.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with global data protection standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement & User Data Policy, and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698).

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide to Us

  • Email & Password Account: If you create an account with email and password, we collect your email address and an authentication credential handled by Firebase Authentication (your password is stored only in hashed form by Google and is never visible to us). You can also use the App as a guest without providing any personal information.
  • Google Authentication Profile: When you sign in using Google Sign-In, we collect your display name, email address, profile picture URL, and unique Firebase User ID (UID) to create and sync your user profile across devices.
  • Community Contributions: When you share tactical takes or comments in the Community Feed, we store your post text, timestamp, and optional attached match prediction.
  • Predictions & Referral Codes: Match predictions (1, X, 2), points accumulated, consecutive streaks, and custom referral invitation codes.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Identifiers & Telemetry: Device model, operating system version (Android), unique advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP address, and network connectivity state.
  • Analytics & Usage Metrics: In-app navigation, feature engagement, prediction submission frequency, and ad impressions collected via Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash Reporting: Stack traces, ANR (Application Not Responding) telemetry, and hardware state at crash time collected via Google Firebase Crashlytics.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To initialize your analyst account and store your predictions in real-time.
  • To compute global and national leaderboard ranks and streak milestones.
  • To reward bonus points when referring friends via your unique invitation link.
  • To monitor and maintain system security, thwart automated abuse or fraudulent score manipulation.
  • To deliver non-intrusive banner and rewarded video ads that support the ongoing maintenance and server costs of the App.
  • To ask for your consent to personalized advertising through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form, shown to users in the EEA, UK and other regions where required. Your choice is stored on your device and you can review or change it at any time in the App under Profile → Ad privacy settings. If you decline, only non-personalized ads are shown. Ads may use your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or opt out of in your Android settings.

5. Data Retention & User Deletion Rights

We retain your user profile, predictions, and accumulated stats for as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership and control over your personal data at all times.

Under Google Play Data Safety requirements and global privacy regulations, you have the right to request full erasure of your account and all associated data at any time without having to install or reinstall the application.

You can delete your account and data directly inside the App under Profile → Account & Data Deletion. Alternatively, use our self-service deletion portal:

Request Account & Data Deletion

6. Children's Privacy

MatchPick Pro is designed and intended for users aged 13 and older (or 18+ depending on sports analysis regulations in your local jurisdiction). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. If we learn that we have inadvertently collected data from a child under 13, we will purge that data immediately.

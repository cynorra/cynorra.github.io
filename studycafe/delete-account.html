Delete your account

Study Cafe: Pomodoro Timer (package: com.cynora.studycafe.pomodoro) · Developer: Cynorra · Türkçe

Option 1: in the app

  1. Open Study Cafe and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm with Delete Account Permanently.

Option 2: by email

If you can no longer open the app, email eren.gp54@gmail.com from the address you registered with, with the subject “Study Cafe account deletion”. We will delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address and password) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your profile document (display name, email, points, study minutes and streaks) in Cloud Firestore.
  • Your leaderboard entry.

What is not deleted

  • Data stored only on your device (session history, settings). It is removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Anonymous analytics and crash reports, which are not linked to your account. Advertising data is held by Google AdMob under Google’s own policies.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. See also our Privacy Policy.

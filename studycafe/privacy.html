Privacy Policy

Study Cafe: Pomodoro Timer (package: com.cynora.studycafe.pomodoro) · Developer: Cynorra · Last updated: October 19, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Study Cafe Android app (“Study Cafe”, “we”) processes and why. By using Study Cafe you accept this policy.

1. Data we process

  • Account data (optional): if you create an account, Firebase Authentication processes your email address and password (the password is handled by Firebase and is never visible to us), and we store the display name you choose.
  • Progress data: points, study minutes, daily streak and longest streak. Stored on your device, and, if you have an account, in Cloud Firestore.
  • Leaderboard: if you have an account, your display name and points are shown to other users on the leaderboard.
  • Study sessions and settings: your session history, timer settings, unlocked cafe items and achievements are stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising data: the app shows banner, interstitial, native and rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which may process your device’s advertising ID, IP address and basic device and usage information.
  • Analytics and crash data: Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics collect anonymous usage events, app instance identifiers, device model, OS version and crash reports so we can fix problems and improve the app. Firebase Remote Config fetches timer and reward settings.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, a session-end reminder is scheduled locally on your device. No push token or other data is sent to a server for it.

2. How we use it

  • to run the timer, track your progress and keep your account signed in;
  • to show the leaderboard;
  • to show ads and let AdMob operate;
  • to measure usage, fix crashes and improve the app.

3. Sharing

We share data only with the infrastructure providers needed to run the app: Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config) and Google AdMob. We do not sell your data. Google’s own privacy policy applies to the data it processes: policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Ads and your choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account exists. You can delete your account and its cloud data at any time in the app (Profile → Delete Account) or by following this page. Data stored only on your device is removed when you uninstall the app.

6. Security

Cloud data is stored on Google Firebase, protected by industry-standard security measures and access rules. No internet transmission is 100% secure.

7. Children

Study Cafe is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Contact us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

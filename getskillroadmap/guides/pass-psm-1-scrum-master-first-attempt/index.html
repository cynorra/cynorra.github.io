1. Scrum Guide 2020: The Three Artifact Commitments

The 2020 revision introduced three explicit, non-negotiable commitments corresponding to each Scrum artifact:

        ├──► Product Backlog   ──────► Commitment: Product Goal
        ├──► Sprint Backlog    ──────► Commitment: Sprint Goal
        └──► Increment         ──────► Commitment: Definition of Done (DoD)

3. High-Frequency Scrum Guide Traps & Anti-Patterns

  • •Who Estimates Product Backlog Items? Exclusively the Developers who will do the work. The Product Owner may influence understanding but never dictates size or velocity.
  • •What is the Sprint Goal? Created collaboratively during Sprint Planning by the entire Scrum Team. It is non-negotiable during the sprint; work may be renegotiated with the Product Owner as more is learned, but the Sprint Goal cannot change.
  • •Who Cancels a Sprint? Only the Product Owner has the authority to cancel a Sprint, and only if the Sprint Goal becomes obsolete.
  • •Daily Scrum Mechanics: It is an internal 15-minute event strictly for Developers. The Scrum Master does not attend to collect status updates or manage assignments.

Team Ceremony Artifacts: Sprint Retrospective & Backlog Refinement Blueprint

Demonstrate agile leadership competence by publishing a verified project delivery workspace in Jira or GitHub Projects:

  • •User Story Architecture: Write 15+ user stories using standard syntax (As a... I want to... So that...) with explicit Given-When-Then Gherkin acceptance criteria.
  • •Sprint Simulation: Execute a 3-sprint simulated backlog with story points estimated using the Fibonacci sequence (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13).
  • •Agile Metrics Dashboard: Publish sprint burn-down charts, velocity tracking sheets showing committed vs completed story points, and a retrospective action log detailing continuous improvement experiments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is the PSM I exam open book?

Yes, technically you can take it from home on your own browser without a webcam proctor. However, with only 45 seconds per question and an 85% passing threshold, searching for answers online will guarantee you run out of time.

Q: Does Scrum.org charge a renewal fee for PSM I like Scrum Alliance does for CSM?

Zero renewal fees. Once you pass PSM I, the certification is permanently valid for life with no mandatory webinars, CEUs, or maintenance payments.

Q: What is the best score threshold on the Scrum Open Assessment before attempting the real exam?

You should consistently score 100% on the official Scrum.org Open Assessment at least 5 consecutive times, completing all 30 questions in under 8 minutes.

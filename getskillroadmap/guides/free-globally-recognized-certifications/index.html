4. How to Bypass Hidden MOOC Payment Walls (Coursera & edX)

Thousands of prospective students abandon world-class courses because commercial platforms design dark patterns suggesting credit card entry is mandatory:

  • •Coursera 'Audit Course' Mode: On individual course landing pages, look for the small, low-contrast text link labeled 'Audit the course' located at the bottom of the enrollment popup. This unlocks all lecture videos, course readings, and discussion forums for $0.
  • •Coursera Financial Aid: Click the 'Financial Aid Available' link next to the enroll button, complete the brief questionnaire explaining your educational necessity, and receive 100% free access to peer-graded assignments and shareable certificates within 16 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do tech companies hire junior UX designers based on the Google certificate alone?

No. Design directors review portfolios, not certificate badges. The value of the Google course is the structure it provides to build 3 case studies; the case studies themselves must be original.

Q: Can I do the whole Google UX course using only Figma instead of Adobe XD?

Yes. Although Course 6 introduces Adobe XD, Google allows students to complete all wireframing, prototyping, and component assignments in Figma.

Q: How can I avoid my portfolio looking like every other Google UX graduate?

Choose messy real-world problems (e.g., specialized medical clinic booking or supply chain logs) instead of common recipe or food delivery app templates.

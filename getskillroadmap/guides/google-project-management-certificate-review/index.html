3. The PMI CAPM Contact Hour Equivalency: Eliminating the $500 Seminar

One of the most valuable aspects of the Google Project Management Certificate is that the Project Management Institute (PMI) officially recognizes it as satisfying the educational prerequisite for the Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM):

  • •Contact Hour Mechanics: To sit for the CAPM exam, PMI mandates 23 hours of structured project management education. Commercial Authorized Training Partners (ATPs) charge $500 to $1,500 for weekend bootcamps designed solely to issue this certificate of completion.
  • •The Cost Arbitrage: By finishing Google's 6-course series on Coursera, candidates earn over 100 qualifying hours of instruction for under $200 (or $0 with Coursera Financial Aid), saving up to $1,300 before booking the Pearson VUE exam.
  • •PMP Professional Development Units (PDUs): For current PMP holders, completing this specialization satisfies up to 100 PDUs, covering entire 3-year recertification cycles in one program.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the Google Project Management certificate directly satisfy the 35 contact hours for PMP?

Yes. The certificate provides over 100 educational hours, which satisfies the 35 contact hours required for PMP and the 23 contact hours required for CAPM.

Q: How long does it realistically take to complete all 6 courses on Coursera?

While Google estimates 6 months at 10 hrs/week, motivated students who dedicate 15-20 hours weekly routinely complete the certificate in 5 to 7 weeks.

Q: Does Google teach Jira in this certificate?

No, Google uses Asana as its primary collaboration tool alongside spreadsheets and Google Workspace docs. However, the workflow fundamentals translate directly to Jira boards.

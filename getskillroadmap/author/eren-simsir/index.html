Eren Şimşir is a software engineer, technical researcher, and the founder of GetSkillRoadmap.com. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering with a concurrent double major in Industrial Engineering from Düzce University, and is currently pursuing his Master of Science in Computer Engineering at Kocaeli University. With a strong analytical focus on systems engineering, software architecture, and skills accreditation, Eren investigates the real hiring return-on-investment (ROI) of global certifications (AWS, Azure, CompTIA, Google, PMI). His mission is to dismantle predatory credentialism, eliminate 'tutorial hell', and provide self-directed engineers with rigorous, proof-of-work project blueprints.

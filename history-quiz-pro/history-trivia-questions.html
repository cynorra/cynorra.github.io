Ancient world: Egypt, Greece and Rome

The Rosetta Stone, key to deciphering ancient Egyptian hieroglyphs, was discovered in which year?

Answer: 1799

Discovered by French soldiers in Rosetta, Egypt, in 1799 during Napoleon's campaign.

The Western Roman Empire fell in which year?

Answer: 476

The last Western Roman emperor was deposed in 476 AD.

Which pharaoh is credited with building the Great Pyramid of Giza?

Answer: Khufu

The Great Pyramid was built for Khufu (Cheops) around 2560 BC during the Fourth Dynasty.

The Rosetta Stone helped scholars decipher which writing system?

Answer: Egyptian hieroglyphs

Jean-François Champollion used the stone's three scripts to decipher hieroglyphs in 1822.

Who discovered the tomb of Tutankhamun in 1922?

Answer: Howard Carter

Howard Carter found the nearly intact tomb in the Valley of the Kings.

Which river was the lifeline of ancient Egyptian civilization?

Answer: The Nile

Annual Nile floods made farming possible in the desert.

Who was the last active ruler of Ptolemaic Egypt?

Answer: Cleopatra VII

Cleopatra died in 30 BC and Egypt became a Roman province.

At the Battle of Kadesh (c. 1274 BC), Ramses II of Egypt fought which empire?

Answer: The Hittites

Kadesh is one of the earliest battles with well-documented tactics, and led to an early peace treaty.

Hammurabi, known for one of the earliest written law codes, was king of which city?

Answer: Babylon

The Code of Hammurabi dates from around 1754 BC.

Which people developed cuneiform, one of the earliest writing systems?

Answer: The Sumerians

Sumerians in Mesopotamia wrote on clay tablets with wedge-shaped marks.

Which seafaring people spread an early phonetic alphabet across the Mediterranean?

Answer: The Phoenicians

The Phoenician alphabet is an ancestor of the Greek and Latin alphabets.

In 490 BC the Athenians defeated a Persian army at which battle?

Answer: Marathon

The Greek victory at Marathon halted Darius I's first invasion of Greece.

King Leonidas and his Spartans made a famous stand in 480 BC at which place?

Answer: Thermopylae

The Greeks delayed Xerxes' vast army at the narrow pass of Thermopylae.

Which Athenian statesman is linked to the Golden Age of Athens and the building of the Parthenon?

Answer: Pericles

Pericles led Athens at the height of its power in the 5th century BC.

Which philosopher tutored the young Alexander the Great?

Answer: Aristotle

Aristotle taught Alexander at the Macedonian court.

In which city did Alexander the Great die in 323 BC?

Answer: Babylon

Alexander died in Babylon at about age 32.

The ancient Olympic Games were held in honor of which god?

Answer: Zeus

The games took place at Olympia, sacred to Zeus.

Which student of Socrates wrote 'The Republic'?

Answer: Plato

Plato founded the Academy in Athens.

Which Roman leader was assassinated on the Ides of March in 44 BC?

Answer: Julius Caesar

A group of senators including Brutus and Cassius killed Caesar.

Who became the first Roman emperor?

Answer: Augustus

Octavian took the name Augustus in 27 BC.

Which Carthaginian general famously crossed the Alps with war elephants to attack Rome?

Answer: Hannibal

Hannibal invaded Italy during the Second Punic War (218 BC).

In which year did Mount Vesuvius bury Pompeii?

Answer: AD 79

The eruption preserved Pompeii and Herculaneum under volcanic ash.

What did the Edict of Milan (313) under Constantine do?

Answer: Granted religious tolerance to Christians

Constantine and Licinius ended the persecution of Christians.

In what year is the fall of the Western Roman Empire traditionally dated?

Answer: AD 476

Odoacer deposed the last western emperor, Romulus Augustulus, in 476.

Which huge amphitheatre in Rome opened in AD 80?

Answer: The Colosseum

The Flavian Amphitheatre hosted gladiator contests and public spectacles.

What was the capital of the Eastern Roman (Byzantine) Empire?

Answer: Constantinople

Constantine refounded Byzantium as Constantinople in 330.

Who is considered the first emperor of a unified China?

Answer: Qin Shi Huang

Qin Shi Huang unified China in 221 BC and began early Great Wall construction.

Medieval world, Renaissance and exploration

Christopher Columbus reached the Americas in which year?

Answer: 1492

Columbus landed in the Caribbean in October 1492.

In what year was Charlemagne crowned Emperor by Pope Leo III?

Answer: 800

Charlemagne's coronation on Christmas Day 800 revived the idea of a western empire.

Which pope called for the First Crusade at Clermont in 1095?

Answer: Urban II

Urban II urged Christians to aid Byzantium and retake Jerusalem.

The Black Death devastated Europe mainly in which century?

Answer: The 14th century

The plague arrived around 1347 and killed a large share of Europe's population.

Joan of Arc fought for France in which conflict?

Answer: The Hundred Years' War

Joan helped lift the siege of Orléans in 1429.

Which Norse explorer is credited with reaching North America around AD 1000?

Answer: Leif Erikson

Leif Erikson reached a land he called Vinland.

Which empire's capital, Constantinople, fell in 1453?

Answer: The Byzantine Empire

Ottoman forces under Mehmed II took the city on 29 May 1453.

Which empire did Genghis Khan found?

Answer: The Mongol Empire

Genghis Khan united the Mongol tribes in 1206.

Mansa Musa, famous for his pilgrimage to Mecca in 1324, ruled which empire?

Answer: The Mali Empire

His lavish journey reportedly affected gold prices in Cairo.

Which Venetian traveler is known for visiting the court of Kublai Khan?

Answer: Marco Polo

Marco Polo's account made China better known in Europe.

The Taj Mahal was built by which Mughal emperor?

Answer: Shah Jahan

Shah Jahan built it as a tomb for his wife Mumtaz Mahal.

Admiral Zheng He led great treasure voyages under which Chinese dynasty?

Answer: The Ming dynasty

Zheng He's fleets sailed to Southeast Asia, India and East Africa in the early 1400s.

Who introduced movable-type printing to Europe around 1450?

Answer: Johannes Gutenberg

Gutenberg's press in Mainz made books cheaper and more widespread.

Who painted the Mona Lisa?

Answer: Leonardo da Vinci

Leonardo painted the portrait in the early 1500s.

Who painted the ceiling of the Sistine Chapel?

Answer: Michelangelo

Michelangelo painted it between 1508 and 1512.

Martin Luther's 95 Theses (1517) helped start which movement?

Answer: The Protestant Reformation

Luther challenged Church practices such as the sale of indulgences.

Christopher Columbus's 1492 voyage was sponsored by which kingdom?

Answer: Spain

Ferdinand and Isabella of Castile and Aragon funded the voyage.

Whose expedition completed the first circumnavigation of the globe (1519-1522)?

Answer: Ferdinand Magellan's

Magellan died in 1521; Juan Sebastián Elcano completed the journey.

Vasco da Gama reached India by sea in 1498 by sailing around which cape?

Answer: Cape of Good Hope

He opened the sea route from Europe to India around Africa.

Nicolaus Copernicus proposed which model of the universe?

Answer: The Sun-centered (heliocentric) model

His 1543 book 'On the Revolutions of the Heavenly Spheres' challenged the old view.

Which conquistador defeated the Aztec Empire in 1521?

Answer: Hernán Cortés

Cortés and his allies took the Aztec capital Tenochtitlan.

Nelson Mandela was released from prison, marking the beginning of the end of apartheid, in which year?

Answer: The Spanish Empire

The Meiji Restoration, which modernized Japan, began in which year?

What name was given to the Allied landings in Normandy on 6 June 1944?

Answer: D-Day

Which country's surrender ended the war in Europe in May 1945?

Answer: Germany

Germany surrendered on 8 May 1945 (V-E Day).

Which two Japanese cities were hit by atomic bombs in August 1945?

Answer: Hiroshima and Nagasaki

The Wall Street Crash of 1929 is associated with the start of which event?

Answer: The Great Depression

The crash triggered a worldwide economic downturn in the 1930s.

Which spacecraft mission carried the first humans to land on the Moon in 1969?

The Cold War was primarily a rivalry between which two superpowers?

Answer: The United States and the Soviet Union

Which leader led the 1930 Salt March against British rule in India?

The United States entered World War I in which year?

Answer: 1917

The attack on Pearl Harbor, which brought the U.S. into World War II, occurred in which year?

Answer: 1941

Apollo 11 landed the first humans on the Moon in which year?

Answer: 1969

Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin landed on the Moon on July 20, 1969.

Winston Churchill became British Prime Minister for the first time in which year?

Answer: 1940

🗑️ Account & Data Deletion Request

History Quiz Pro (Package: com.cynorra.historyapp)

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and Global Data Protection Regulations (GDPR & KVKK), users of History Quiz Pro have the right to request the complete deletion of their account and all associated personal and gameplay data.

1. Deletion via In-App Options

You can instantly delete your account and wipe your local and cloud data without waiting:

  • Open History Quiz Pro on your device.
  • Navigate to the Profile (Profilim) tab.
  • Scroll down and tap "🗑️ Hesabımı ve Tüm Verilerimi Sil" (Delete Account & Reset Data).
  • Confirm the prompt. Your Google account link, stats, achievements, and leaderboard entries will be deleted immediately.

2. Deletion Request via Web / Email

If you no longer have the app installed or prefer to request deletion via web, you can submit your request directly by emailing our support team:

✉️ Submit Account Deletion Request via Email

3. What Data Will Be Deleted?

Upon receiving your request, the following data associated with your account will be permanently erased within 30 days:

  • Google Authentication credentials & profile ID.
  • Firestore Leaderboard rankings, total score, and correct/wrong stats.
  • Current daily streak and unlocked career achievements.
  • Firebase Push notification tokens.
✅ Data Retention Policy: Anonymized, aggregated app analytics (such as total quizzes played across all users) that cannot be linked back to your identity may be retained for technical statistics.

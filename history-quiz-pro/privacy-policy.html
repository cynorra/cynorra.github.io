Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cynorra ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use the History Quiz Pro mobile application (the "App"), available on Google Play under package name com.cynorra.historyapp.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account information — if you sign in with Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo as provided by your Google account, so we can create and manage your player profile.

1.2 Information collected automatically

  • Gameplay data — quiz scores, streaks, achievements, and leaderboard rankings, stored via Firebase Firestore.
  • Analytics data — app usage, screen views, and interaction events, collected via Firebase Analytics, to help us understand how the App is used and improve it.
  • Crash and diagnostic data — device model, OS version, and crash logs, collected via Firebase Crashlytics, to help us identify and fix bugs.
  • Push notification tokens — a device token via Firebase Cloud Messaging, used to send you optional notifications (e.g. daily quiz reminders).
  • Advertising identifiers — Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other technical identifiers to serve and measure ads. Where required (e.g. in the EEA/UK), we request your consent for personalized advertising via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any such data is collected.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's quiz, scoring, and leaderboard features.
  • To personalize your experience and save your progress across sessions/devices.
  • To display and measure advertising through Google AdMob.
  • To analyze usage trends and improve app performance and content.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes and technical issues.
  • To send you optional push notifications, which you can disable at any time in your device settings.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy governing how it handles data:

  • Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging) — Google LLC
  • Google AdMob — Google LLC
  • Google Sign-In — Google LLC
  • Wikipedia — Wikimedia Foundation. The "Today in History" card loads a public list of events for today's date from Wikipedia. Your device connects directly to Wikipedia servers, which receive your IP address and the request language; we do not send any account or gameplay data.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above, as necessary for them to perform their functions (hosting, analytics, advertising, authentication), and only to the extent required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and gameplay data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see "Your Rights" below); some anonymized or aggregated analytics data may be retained after deletion.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights & Account/Data Deletion

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent for personalized advertising at any time. You can delete your account and data directly inside the App under Profile ➔ Delete Account, or submit a web-based deletion request on our dedicated Account & Data Deletion Request Page.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures (including those provided by Firebase and Google Cloud) to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

What is History Quiz Pro?

History Quiz Pro is a free Android app that serves a fresh set of history quiz questions every day. Players earn XP, level up and rank on a live leaderboard.

Which history topics does it cover?

Choose a country (Turkey, the United States, the United Kingdom or Global) or a focused topic: the Ancient World (Egypt, Greece and Rome), the Medieval World and Exploration, Revolutions, Wars and the Modern World. For Turkish exam preparation there are three units: Early Turkic and Seljuk History, Ottoman History, and the History of the Turkish Revolution and Atatürk's Principles.

The Daily tab also shows an "On This Day in History" card with events that happened on today's date.

Frequently asked questions

In what language are the questions?

US, UK and Global questions are in English. The Turkey category is in Turkish. The interface is available in English and Turkish.

Is it free?

Yes. It contains ads, including optional rewarded ads that unlock a 50/50 hint.

Do I need an account?

No. Google sign-in is optional. You can delete your account and data from the Profile screen.
